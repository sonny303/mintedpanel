# Group Contract Status Workflow Spike

**Date:** 2026-09-26

**Status:** Source and schema decision record; operator walkthrough and payer pathway remain unverified

**Base:** `sonny303/mintedpanel` GitHub `main` at `06261a400261203bbb2b03e40a9b73adc7e0c2fc`

**Scope:** Planning only. No schema, data, route, configuration, portal, or product changes are authorized by this document.

## Decision

Build a distinct group agreement workflow and report. Preserve the four separate facts below and never derive a group agreement from a provider case, an enrollment fact, a payer target, or a billing-readiness result.

| Fact                           | Canonical evidence                                                                                                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Group contracting              | Agreement version, exact coverage, events, and source evidence proposed here                                                 | Whether a legal group/TIN has an agreement with a payer for an explicitly covered state/product/location.                                          |
| Clinician credentialing        | `credential_cases.case_status` and `case_status_history`                                                                     | The canonical eight-state provider case workflow. Keep this state machine; do not add a competing provider status.                                 |
| Clinician enrollment           | Product/group/facility/state scope and its revision/proof, where captured; legacy `enrollment_facts` remain coarser          | A payer’s disposition for one clinician relationship. An approved case or an enrollment fact does not prove every product, facility, or agreement. |
| Activation / billing clearance | A conservative, dated evaluation of agreement coverage plus applicable clinician, enrollment, facility, and license evidence | A decision for a scoped relationship on an assessment date. It is not a synonym for “contracted” or “case approved.”                               |

**Recommendation:** add an append-only group-agreement model with versioned coverage links; keep the legacy `contracts` table as a read-only compatibility/source record. Reuse WP1.1’s payer-product identity, explicit group grants, and restricted report boundary where they apply. Do not reuse its provider-enrollment scope or proof rows to assert a group agreement. Do not duplicate its payer catalog, access model, or provider-enrollment read model.

**Pilot shape (provisional):** one selected legal group/TIN, payer, state, and actual product/network; one first-agreement work item followed by one explicitly selected clinician-add path. Store a single agreement version with explicit coverage links, then link each provider-add transaction to its own case and facility. The actual payer, portal/channel, whether the later add is a roster or individual application, operator owner, and allowed pre-effective work are **not selected** until redacted first-agreement and subsequent-add examples are reviewed.

Review of those redacted documents must resolve whether the signed agreement is one agreement or separate agreements; which products/networks and states it names; the legal entity and TIN actually signing; any facility/location limits; signature, effective, expiry, amendment, and termination terms; and whether the later clinician add uses an individual application, delegated roster, or another acknowledged route. Do not infer these dimensions from payer form names or current group settings.

## To-be agreement, event, and evidence contract

### Grain and options

The report’s coverage row is one **agreement version × legal group/TIN × payer × state × actual product/network coverage**. If one signed agreement covers several products, store one agreement document/version and several explicit coverage links; never duplicate the signed agreement merely to fill matrix cells. If a payer confirms that coverage is agreement-wide rather than product-specific, represent that explicit scope as `agreement_only`; it is not a wildcard or a guessed product. Unknown product coverage is `needs_mapping`, never effective.

| Option                                                                                  | Result                                                                                                                                                                        | Decision                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep one `contracts` row per group/payer/state and add product or duplicate per version | Conflicts with the live uniqueness; duplicating a signed agreement for each product/version causes divergent dates/evidence and hides amendments.                             | Reject.                                                              |
| Enlarge/re-key `contracts` into product/agreement grain                                 | Alters a long-lived service/API and compatibility contract; old status and date semantics are already weak and no live rows provide a safe conversion example.                | Reject as first slice. Keep the table readable for legacy consumers. |
| Add agreement series/version plus explicit coverage child rows                          | Supports one document across many products/states without treating group targets as proof; preserves the old table and separates intent, agreement, and clinician enrollment. | Recommend.                                                           |

**Proposed additive model (private, tenant-bound):**

| Relation                       | Grain and contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `contract_agreements`          | Stable agreement-series ID (`agreement_series_id` UUID) with `org_id`, `group_id`, and `payer_id`; no uniqueness constraint on group+payer, so independent agreements can coexist. Create a series only when reviewed documents show a distinct agreement family.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `contract_agreement_versions`  | Immutable executed-instrument ID (`agreement_version_id` UUID), created only when the executed document/evidence is accepted. Stores the exact signing legal-name/TIN snapshot, signed/effective/expiry terms, predecessor/supersession links and their supported date boundary, and source-document version/hash. A pending amendment is not an executed version and cannot change current coverage.                                                                           |
| `contract_agreement_revisions` | Append-only operational/event ID (`agreement_revision_id` UUID) for each queue/workflow change: event kind, stage, dates, owner, next action, blocker, dependency-policy version, actor, business date, observed timestamp, reason, and the executed version in force at that moment. A pending amendment is recorded here while the prior executed version remains current. The queue reads the latest revision; these IDs are never counted as agreements or signed versions. |
| `contract_agreement_coverage`  | Immutable links from either an executed `agreement_version_id` or a proposed `agreement_revision_id` (exactly one target) to state, optional `private.payer_products.id`, explicit coverage kind (`product` or payer-confirmed `agreement_only`), and facility scope only when evidence says location-limited. Proposed links never authorize coverage. No wildcard/null inference.                                                                                             |
| `contract_agreement_evidence`  | Immutable evidence item linked to an operational revision or executed version: exact private document version/payer reference, evidence kind, hash/version, supported fields, actor, observed time, and business date. Storage bucket/ACL reuse must be verified in Slice 1; do not expose reusable or public URLs.                                                                                                                                                             |

Reuse `private.payer_products` and `private.group_product_targets` from E6.13. No parallel payer catalog or group-product target. Because E6.13’s scope/proof model is provider-specific, group agreement evidence stays separate; its group grants and verified audience context remain the client authorization source.

These identifiers are not interchangeable: `agreement_series_id` identifies the continuing agreement relationship; `agreement_version_id` identifies one immutable executed instrument; `agreement_revision_id` identifies one append-only operational event/snapshot. A pending amendment creates revisions under the series but no new executed version and does not displace the existing effective version. After the amendment is executed, create a new version that explicitly supersedes its predecessor or the specific coverage links the document replaces.

For a report or fill `as_of_date`, consider only executed, evidence-verified versions with a coverage link matching the exact group/TIN, payer, state, product/network, and any location scope. The version's verified effective date must be on/before `as_of_date`; its verified expiry/termination date, when present, must be after it. A verified supersession boundary excludes the predecessor for that exact coverage key on and after the boundary, even if the successor has since expired or terminated; never revive a superseded version by falling back. A future-effective successor does not supersede its predecessor before its supported boundary. If termination or supersession is asserted but the supported date is missing or conflicting, return `manual_review` for that coverage key. For an assessment before a later supported termination/supersession date, use the prior supported version/evidence valid on `as_of_date`. Select the latest-effective eligible version only within the same series and exact coverage key. If independent series overlap or the document does not establish which coverage was superseded, return conflict/unknown for manual review; never choose by insertion time. The API requires a date-only `as_of_date`, returns that date plus `evaluated_at`, and the UI defaults it to the selected organization's business date.

### Agreement state and event rules

Keep **stage**, **evidence quality**, **coverage**, **dependency**, and **calendar dates** as separate fields. Each queue action appends a new `agreement_revision_id`; each accepted signed instrument has a separate `agreement_version_id`. Minimal derived stage vocabulary:

`targeted → outreach → application_in_progress → submitted → negotiation → pending_signature → executed → effective`

Terminal/branch outcomes: `declined`, `withdrawn`, `expired`, `terminated`, `superseded`, and `needs_mapping` (legacy/ambiguous source only). These are not free-form labels from `status_configs`.

- `targeted`, outreach, work owner, blocker, and next action may be operator-recorded with an audit event. A `submitted` event requires a submission receipt/reference or a witnessed send marker. `executed` requires an immutable signed-document version and signed date. `effective` requires evidence that supports the actual payer/group/product/state and effective date; a contract-stated date may be recorded as documented terms, but “payer-confirmed” must be explicitly sourced as such.
- Store `business_date` separately from `observed_at`. Never use row `created_at` as intake, submission, signature, payer acknowledgement, or effective date. Acknowledgement means receipt only, not approval. Agreement effective date can be future; it is not effective before that date.
- Evidence supports only listed fields. Keep document version/hash, issuer/source, `verified_by/at`, and field-level support. An event without adequate evidence remains recorded but is `unverified`; it cannot yield client `effective`, agent-ready, or activation assertions.
- A proposed amendment/renewal appends a work revision and candidate coverage; it cannot change effective coverage. Once executed, mint an immutable new version and exact coverage links. Supersession is per exact coverage key unless the signed document explicitly replaces a larger scope; its supported boundary permanently excludes the predecessor from that date, including after the successor later expires or terminates. No implicit predecessor fallback or reactivation is allowed; renewed/restored coverage requires a new executed version with explicit links. Termination or expiry stops current coverage from its supported date while retaining historical versions and provider decisions. Reopening appends a revision; never rewrites old evidence.
- Corrections append a correction revision with reason and actor. Only authorized staff may correct; no ordinary UPDATE/DELETE to revision/evidence history. Each revision and its evidence/coverage snapshot commit atomically; the latest valid revision drives the queue, while prior revisions remain visible as history.

### Dependency and permission contract

Store a versioned, payer/product/state/transaction-specific rule result with `can_prepare`, `can_submit`, and `can_activate` each `allowed | blocked | unknown`, plus rule version, evaluated inputs, reason, and evidence. Unsupported pathway = `unknown` and a manual-review stop for the affected action, not a guessed permission and not a universal block on unrelated enrollment work.

| Capability     | Minimum rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `can_prepare`  | May be true before execution only when an observed payer/pathway rule permits it and an exact case, group/TIN, payer product, state, facility, form transaction, and SOP are selected. An unverified agreement never supplies group values by fallback.                                    |
| `can_submit`   | Requires a supported route, current prerequisites, final rendered draft review, and a separately authorized human submit action. A prepared form or extension fill never implies sent.                                                                                                     |
| `can_activate` | Derived only when the applicable agreement coverage and individual enrollment evidence are verified/effective on the assessment date, required group/location links are covered, and any other payer-specific prerequisites pass. It does not assert every location or product is covered. |

Keep three distinct authorizations: prepare a draft, submit externally, and assert activation/billing clearance. The first automation objective stays a reviewable draft. Signature, attestation, and external submission remain human decisions. Do not wire `can_activate` directly to billing release; WP1.2 owns its conservative evaluation.

3. ### Report contract and denominators

The matrix/detail/export row is one coverage tuple. Keep agreement document/version counts distinct from coverage-cell counts and provider work counts; aggregate only children the active staff organization or explicit client group grants authorize.

| Field group     | Internal report                                                                                                                                     | Restricted client summary                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity        | Group and legal-party revision (no tax ID); payer; state; actual product/network or explicit agreement-only scope; agreement/version; source target | Granted group; payer; state; published product/network/scope; published agreement stage/version label only                   |
| Agreement       | Stage; executed/effective/expiry/termination business dates; predecessor/amendment; source and field support; evidence state; verified actor/time   | Published safe stage and explicitly approved dates; no document or source link; a date appears only with published evidence. |
| Operations      | Owner, next action, due/last-checked time, safe/internal blocker, payer reference and evidence trail                                                | Published client-safe blocker and owner category only; no staff name, raw note, or private reference.                        |
| Dependency/work | Rule version and `can_prepare/can_submit/can_activate`; explicit location coverage; scoped provider-add drilldown                                   | Only explicitly published group-safe summary; no draft scopes, private dependency notes, or evidence files.                  |
| Filters/export  | Group, payer, actual product, state, stage, owner, blocker, effective/expiry window; bounded export through identical authorization                 | Granted groups only; filters and export are server constrained to current grants and publication state.                      |

**Count definitions:**

- Agreement series count = distinct `agreement_series_id`; executed agreement count/version count = distinct `agreement_version_id`; operational activity count = distinct `agreement_revision_id`. These are different denominators. Coverage count = distinct `(agreement_version_id, state, coverage_kind, payer_product_id, facility_scope)` links. Never count each product link as a separate signed agreement or count work revisions as agreements.
- A provider-enrollment **relationship row** exists only for an explicitly captured WP1.1 scope with exact `(provider_id, group_id, product_id, facility_id, state)` identity. A `credential_case` remains at `(provider_id, group_id, payer_id, state)` and can link to multiple explicitly captured scopes. Count `relationship_count = count(distinct scope_id)`, `case_count = count(distinct case_id)`, and `clinician_count = count(distinct provider_id)` across the full selected filter set—not by summing row subtotals. A clinician in two groups is one distinct clinician and two or more group relationships. Cases not linked to an exact product/facility scope are a separate `unmapped_case_count`; do not multiply them across payer-product targets.
- Require an explicit date-only `as_of_date`; evaluate the latest scope revision and evidence known by `evaluated_at` against that date. Assign exactly one `relationship_bucket` per in-scope `scope_id`, in this precedence order:

  1. `terminated` only when a supported termination date is on/before `as_of_date`. A termination label alone does not establish the date. If a termination is asserted but its date is missing or conflicting, use `manual_review`. If its supported date is after `as_of_date`, evaluate from the prior supported status and evidence valid on that earlier date; do not apply a later termination retroactively. If no supported prior state exists, use `manual_review`.
  2. `not_pursuing` if the current scope revision is not pursuing; `denied` if the latest supported payer outcome is denied.
  3. For a current `approved` scope, first require verified current approval evidence/source fingerprint. If absent/stale/conflicting, use `approved_unverified`. If the payer-specific rule requires a group contract and exact effective coverage is absent for `as_of_date`, use `blocked_by_contract`. If the verified effective date is later than `as_of_date`, use `approved_future_effective`. Use `blocked_other` when a known non-contract prerequisite (such as an inactive required facility or lapsed required license) fails; use `effective` only when evidence, applicable agreement coverage, provider/group/facility linkage, and every required active license pass.
  4. Otherwise use `payer_acknowledged` when verified acknowledgement date is on/before `as_of_date`; `submitted` when verified submission date is on/before it. If the captured status says submitted/acknowledged but the date/source needed to establish that milestone is absent or conflicting, use `manual_review`, not a lower progress bucket.
  5. For not-yet-submitted work, use `blocked_by_contract` when the supported rule requires exact current contract coverage for the next required step and it is missing, expired, superseded, or unresolved. Keep `can_prepare` as a separate column; it may still be true even when this exclusive bucket is `blocked_by_contract`.
  6. Use `ready_to_submit` only when the selected transaction and draft satisfy all known prerequisites and `can_submit=allowed`; `preparable` when `can_prepare=allowed` but the draft is incomplete/not ready; `blocked_other` for a known non-contract blocker; `manual_review` for unknown identity, evidence, overlap, or payer rule; and `not_started` only when the exact scope and pathway are known but no work milestone exists.

  Earlier payer events do not disappear when a later contract expires: the relationship bucket reflects the actual payer milestone, with the current contract blocker shown separately. `can_prepare/can_submit/can_activate`, blocker, case status, and relationship bucket remain distinct fields. An unknown rule is never `ready_to_submit` or `effective`.

- Contract status, credentialing status, enrollment status, and activation result have separate columns and denominators. Never sum them into a single completion percentage.

### Authorization and publication

- E6.12 `resolve_enrollment_context` returns `staffOrgs[].role`, `staffOrgs[].reportStaff`, `staffOrgs[].clientManage`, and client group IDs under `clientOrgs[].groups[]`; these are the actual current context fields. `reportStaff` is true only when active `private.internal_staff.staff_role` matches the caller’s same-org membership role (`admin` or `specialist`). `clientManage` is true only for an `admin` membership with an active same-org `admin` allowlist entry. It is not itself agreement-publication authority; Slice 1 uses it as part of the verified admin check, then applies the agreement-specific operation policy below.
- **Slice 1 operation matrix:**

  | Verified context required by the service-only RPC                                                                                             | Allowed agreement operations                                                                                                                                                                                | Explicitly denied                                                                                                                                                                           |
  | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | `audience='staff'`; selected same-org `staffOrgs[]` has `role='specialist'` and `reportStaff=true`                                            | Create a work draft; append target, outreach, payer-contact, application, receipt, owner, blocker, due-date, and other non-attestation work revisions; attach/read staff-only evidence needed for the work. | Attest that an agreement is executed/effective; publish/revoke client summaries; assign client group grants.                                                                                |
  | `audience='staff'`; selected same-org `staffOrgs[]` has `role='admin'`, `reportStaff=true`, and `clientManage=true`                           | Specialist work operations, plus attest/review executed and effective evidence; publish/revoke the explicitly allowlisted client-safe agreement summary.                                                    | Any assertion without exact evidence/coverage; signing or submitting externally through this UI. An admin records a witnessed signature; this does not make the admin the agreement signer. |
  | `audience='client'`; selected `clientOrgs[]` has active client context and an exact `groups[].groupId` match                                  | Read only a currently published, allowlisted summary for that granted group.                                                                                                                                | All draft, evidence, version, event, publish, revoke, and private-document operations; every client write route/RPC is denied.                                                              |
  | Missing/mismatched membership or staff allowlist, stale/revoked context, wrong audience/org/group, anonymous actor, or forged actor transport | None.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | All agreement reads/writes not authorized above; fail closed even when the UI hides the action.                                                                                             |

  This applies E6.12’s defaults: specialist may draft; internal admin may publish or revoke. Execution/effective attestation is assigned to that same internal-admin boundary for Slice 1. Do not invent `canDraft`, `canAttest`, or `canPublish` as E6.12 capability keys; the service maps verified `role` plus `reportStaff` (and, for admin operations, `clientManage`) to this agreement-specific matrix. E6.13’s `private.e613_require_context` already uses `reportStaff`, then requires `role='admin'` plus `clientManage` for its admin-only path; Slice 1 should preserve that check shape without treating `clientManage` alone as publication authority.

- Each browser route authenticates the bearer JWT, obtains actor ID server-side, resolves the selected `staff` or `client` context through `resolve_enrollment_context`, and applies the operation matrix plus exact org/group predicates on every request. Agreement RPCs remain unexposed to browser direct calls: private tables deny `anon`/`authenticated`, RPC `EXECUTE` is granted only to `service_role`, and each service-only RPC requires non-null `p_actor_user_id` set by the server, re-resolves context, rejects forged body/query/metadata actor values, and writes audit in the same transaction. Client write attempts and direct anon/authenticated RPC/table calls must return denial with no partial rows; clients read only through the authenticated report API with a fresh exact-group grant check.
- Client reads require verified client audience and an explicit `client_group_grants` group match on every request. Existing E6.12/E6.13 access context is the permission source; do not invent a second report permission or infer a grant from organization membership.
- Group agreement evidence is not a provider-enrollment proof publication. Keep full agreement files and negotiation facts staff-only. Launch the first pilot staff-only. If a client report is later included, record publish/revoke as immutable agreement revision events and return an allowlisted group summary through the same audience/grant/report API boundary; hide withdrawn or stale assertions immediately on fresh read/export. No second permission model, public link, reusable signed URL, cacheable private response, or client ability to infer ungranted group existence.
- Default pilot may launch staff-only. Client publication remains off until exact fields, grant behavior, revoke behavior, direct API denial, export parity, and hosted runtime are verified. E6.13 schema presence alone is not proof of those behaviors.

## Case-bound form and provider-add context

After panel PR #425 is resolved, reconcile to its final server contract. Required fill transaction envelope:

```text
caseId + transactionType + groupId + payerId + payerProductId + state
+ facilityId(s) + agreementSeriesId + agreementVersionId|null
+ agreementRevisionId + asOfDate + SOP/form-map version
```

The three agreement identifiers have fixed meanings in the envelope: stable `agreementSeriesId`; the immutable executed `agreementVersionId` selected for this coverage key and `asOfDate` (nullable only when a documented rule permits preparation before execution); and the append-only `agreementRevisionId` carrying the work/policy decision used for this fill. Never substitute one ID for another. A pending amendment’s revision may accompany the prior executed version; it does not make the proposed version current. The panel server re-reads the case under authenticated org scope; proves provider, group, payer, state and selected facility relationships; proves the product target and agreement coverage; resolves selected-group TIN, group NPI and signer only for approved mapped fields; evaluates the payer/pathway rule; and returns a short-lived authenticated no-store fill context. Case ID is the business binding, not a logging-only value. The selected facility must remain the user’s chosen valid location, including a secondary location; it must never silently substitute a primary facility.

The extension requests and consumes this context only for the selected case/task. Missing, stale, mismatched, ambiguous, revoked, or unsupported context stops affected fields and leaves a reviewable draft with explicit unresolved reasons. No primary-group/TIN fallback, product inference, raw-value fallback for unknown transforms, submission, signature, attestation, or activation assertion. Logs carry transaction/correlation identifiers only; no agreement text, TIN, or PHI.

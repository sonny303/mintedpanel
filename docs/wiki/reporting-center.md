# Reporting Center

_Updated for: pre-E6 baseline (2026-07-19). Pages describe the shipped app; target-state notes are marked with their epic._

Cross-org reports, grouped.

## Today (pre-E6)

A report registry with the Portfolio report and expiring-credentials report.

## Target state _(lands with E6.6)_

Four groups: **Performance** (Portfolio, Launches report) ·
**Credentialing** (Denials rollup — provider-first, payer-pivotable ·
Expiring credentials) · **Compliance** (Audit Log) · **Intake** (Inbound
leads, with badge). Occasional counts (facilities with no providers,
locations per group) are reports here, not screen widgets.

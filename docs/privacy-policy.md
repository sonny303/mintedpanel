# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026
**Applies to:** the Minted Panel web application and the Minted Panel Workbench browser extension (together, "the Service").

Minted Panel, LLC ("we," "us") provides Minted Panel, a credentialing and contracting management platform for healthcare practice groups. This policy explains what information the Service handles and how.

## Information we handle

- **Account information.** Name and email, used to sign you in.
- **Provider credentialing data.** Information your organization enters about its healthcare providers: names, dates of birth, professional identifiers (NPI, CAQH ID, license numbers), education, addresses, and the last four digits of Social Security numbers only. This data belongs to your organization and is visible only within it.
- **Activity records.** Actions taken in the Service (form fills, submissions, status changes, edits), logged with who performed them and when.

## How the browser extension works

The Minted Panel Workbench extension fills insurance payer enrollment forms using your organization's provider data.

- Works only after you sign in.
- Retrieves your organization's provider data over an encrypted connection and enters it into supported payer portal forms.
- Writes to forms only. It does not read, collect, or transmit page content.
- Never submits a form. You review and submit every application yourself.
- Session data is cleared when you close your browser.
- Records each fill and confirmed submission to your organization's audit trail.

## How we use information

We use this information only to operate the Service: signing you in, filling forms you request, keeping your organization's records, and maintaining audit trails.

We do not sell it. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. We do not transfer it to third parties except the service providers below, or as required by law.

## Service providers

We run the Service on established infrastructure providers, currently Vercel (application hosting) and Supabase (database and authentication). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. Data is encrypted in transit.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain your organization's data while it uses the Service. If your organization ends its use, we delete its data within 30 days of termination or a written deletion request, unless required by law to keep it longer.

## Not a HIPAA business associate

The Service does not create, receive, or transmit patient medical records or other protected health information. We are not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate with respect to the data described in this policy.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

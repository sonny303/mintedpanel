### Contract invariants

- The existing four-part `credential_cases` key `(provider, group, payer, state)` remains operational and unchanged. Canonical `case_status` and `case_status_history` remain canonical. Existing `enrollment_facts` remain history and are never automatically copied into product or facility approval.
- `payer_products` are stable identities beneath a payer. Internal admins curate them manually; this work seeds no invented products. A `group_product_target` records intended group/product/state coverage only. No target is enrollment proof. No row means **No record**, never “all products” or “all states.”
- An `enrollment_scope` has a unique, concrete, non-null `(org, provider, group, product, facility, state)` identity. Incomplete captures never create nullable canonical scopes; they remain in a staff-only unresolved source queue derived from cases/facts. Provider-facility assignment proves facility practice; provider-group assignment proves group relationship; neither proves payer enrollment.
- Each scope has append-only immutable revisions. A revision owns status, intake/complete-to-submit/submitted/payer-acknowledged/effective/termination dates, payer reference, client-safe blocker, and owner (`Minted`, `Client`, `Payer`, `Complete`, `Unassigned`) independently of case status. Date-only values and observed timestamps are distinct. `created_at` never infers intake.
- Reapplication creates a new small cycle number on a new revision and retains every prior date, reference, source, and publication lineage. Corrections are new revisions or correction events, never silent mutation. Retro is tri-state: `unknown`, `not_supported`, or `documented`, with a bounded day/date value and supporting evidence. It never implies timely filing or billable readiness.

## Source, publication, and missingness model

Each `enrollment_scope_sources` link binds one source snapshot: a `credential_case` or `enrollment_fact`. A revision may have multiple source links; each must match org, provider, group, payer, and state. The case fingerprint covers exactly case id, org/provider/group/payer/state, `case_status`, submitted/approved/confirmed-or-expected-effective/termination dates, payer reference, payer individual/group IDs, contract executed date, and sorted full `case_facilities` IDs. The fact fingerprint covers exactly fact id, org/provider/group/payer/state, effective date, payer-issued ID, and `expired_at`. Recompute on publish/read/export; missing or deleted source invalidates the verified assertion. Preserve identity and snapshot so case purge neither cascades historical provenance nor blocks existing purge; validate source existence at bind/read.

Any correction, expiry, or fingerprint change marks dependent assertions **Needs verification**. The last approved publication may remain as a safe scope shell with the stale label, while verified fields and proof are withheld. Drafts, unresolved scopes, and never-published records remain private. A retracted publication has no downloadable proof and yields **No published enrollment** when no other approved publication remains. Provenance retains scope revision, source fingerprint, actor, time, and reason. Report capture never writes back to case status.

Summary publication and proof publication are distinct. A pending summary, safe blocker, and owner may publish without approval proof. A scope marked Approved/current effective/retro is verified only when applicable proof is published. A license PSV supports only a license assertion; a payer approval letter or roster confirmation supports product, location, effective date, or retro only when the document explicitly states those fields; an acknowledgement supports receipt milestone only. An internal admin reviews scope and sensitive contents; a separate redacted upload may be made for clients while the original remains private.

Proof publication binds an exact immutable document version, SHA-256 digest, scope revision, supported field list, and evidence kind. No overwrite is allowed and the digest is validated before serving. Publication is append-only with `publication_events`; proof retraction immediately denies new downloads and removes current proof assurance, while bytes already downloaded cannot be revoked.

For a client, a missing matrix cell reads **No published enrollment**. Drafts, unresolved scopes, and targets without scope records remain private and do not become client-visible pending proof. A stale last published summary may show **Needs verification** plus a labeled historical prior status, with current verified fields and proof withheld. A retracted publication has no current proof assurance; if no other approved publication remains, the cell reads **No published enrollment**. A client-visible blocker is only the explicitly captured client-safe blocker on a published, authorized summary DTO.

Rows and product columns are the union of the authorized active, verified, non-test, non-reference provider-group cohort and authorized valid published scope records, including reference/inactive/terminated clinicians when a valid scope exists; those rows carry visible lifecycle labels. Product columns come from targets plus matching published scope products. Historical mode adds historical, retracted, and superseded records only where the selected audience is authorized. Published test rows are blocked from client DTOs. Valid completed enrollments cannot disappear. The union never manufactures a provider, product, location, or enrollment assertion.

## Access and authentication contract

Internal staff authorization requires an operator-maintained `private.internal_staff` entry plus valid org membership with an admin or specialist role; it is never self-created admin access. Client access uses service-only records for a verified auth user and org plus explicit child provider-group grants. Clients do not get `memberships` rows. Approved defaults: internal admins assign selected groups; specialists may draft; internal admins publish or revoke client summaries and proof. These are product decisions approved for WP1.1; the child epic remains `status: draft` until its requirements PR is merged.

Client invite flow: create a hashed, single-use, seven-day token; verify email equality at claim; atomically claim it, create service access and child grants, and append an audit row. Do not auto-claim staff invites. Generate a URL only; outbound email is out of scope.

The auth context must resolve before staff shell/hooks mount. The current `__root.tsx` sends non-members to `NoOrgScreen`; `auth-store` auto-claims invites on login; `useLandingRedirect` routes to Cases/Portfolio; `AppShell` mounts global search; and `Sidebar` mounts internal case/status hooks. Add a guarded audience/context mode before those hooks run. Preserve all six internal nav links. Client context is allowed only Reporting Center and this report. `/reporting/enrollment-explorer` and its registry entry are reserved integration contracts: E6.12 proves context and synthetic denied-route behavior, while the later matrix/drawer/CSV slice ships the route, registry, and report UI. No second report route is introduced.

Auth/org/grant revision changes cancel in-flight work and clear cached data and drawers. Dual capability is valid; a user explicitly chooses staff or client context and organization, and switching context clears state before hooks remount. A stale or invalid context fails closed until the chooser resolves. Protected edits to `__root.tsx`, auth store, landing/login, AppShell, and Sidebar require the trust-boundary PR and a narrow browser-to-API exception. Actor-capability enforcement applies to every protected request, including direct REST/RPC/storage and auth-only training surfaces, regardless of page or current UI; legitimate internal request/response shapes remain unchanged. Report staff/browser reads use that API; existing member reads and extension/API contracts remain unchanged. Every service-role read independently scopes org and grants.

Before external tokens or client UAT, close effective grants for direct REST and RPC access: `PUBLIC EXECUTE`, storage policies, and existing auth-only functions require positive and negative tests. Local synthetic identities are permitted; source-confirmed global auth-only functions and native denial regressions are required before hosted activation. Coordinate the existing staging-repair owner; this contract grants no automatic hosted repair permission.

The report uses current tokens, `PageHeader`, `StatusPill`, dialogs, and table patterns. Mobbin references ([045edb7a](https://mobbin.com/screens/045edb7a-6c61-48ba-82bc-acc794b801c1), [79a31934](https://mobbin.com/screens/79a31934-4ca2-4700-86a5-c180fa735404)) are interaction references only.

URL filters are `org`, `group`, `state`, `facility`, `product`, `discipline`, `status`, and `search`. The server authorizes the cohort before applying filters. The authoritative source is `providers.taxonomy_code` through the current closed NUCC map in `src/lib/providerTaxonomy.ts` (PT base/specializations, PTA, and dietitian). Add a pure `src/lib/providerDiscipline.ts` crosswalk, reviewed against the official NUCC catalog at implementation, for PT, PTA, OT, OTA, and SLP; blank or unrecognized codes are `Unknown`, and another recognized discipline such as dietitian is `Other`. Loose credentials never infer licensed discipline. Any OT/OTA/SLP picker additions are a narrow capture dependency, with no new discipline table and no invented codes.

The matrix is sectioned by group/state, with provider rows and bounded payer-product columns. Multiple locations show a count badge and drawer picker; no scalar location reduction. The lazy drawer contains scope/revision status, precise dates, payer reference, retro tri-state/evidence, owner/blocker, source timeline, publication state, and proof controls. Unscoped legacy summaries appear only through a scoped group-safe DTO, never anonymously.

Distinct states are required for no org, no grants, empty cohort, query error, unverified source, and loading. The default view is the union of the active, verified, non-test, non-reference cohort and authorized valid published non-test scopes, with lifecycle labels; historical mode is explicit. Use keyset pagination over 50 distinct provider keys with stable tie-breaker and all matching records for page clinicians. Keep payloads under the proposed 1 MiB stable-page target with bounded columns and horizontal virtualization. Mobile is list then drilldown, with keyboard focus and drawer escape/focus return.

Proposed targets (unproven): 3,000 clinicians × 20 products with multi-locations; controlled API p95 ≤500 ms; derivation ≤50 ms; no steady-scroll long task >50 ms. Label local, preview, and hosted evidence separately.

## CSV contract

CSV is a client-safe, long-form export of actual matching authorized scope records using the detail view’s server allowlist and filters. Fixed column order: provider name, NPI, discipline, group, payer, product, state, facility, status, publication state, intake date, complete-to-submit date, submitted date, payer-acknowledged date, approved date, effective date, termination date, current application cycle, payer reference, retro type, retro value, retro basis, client-safe blocker, owner, reviewed-as-of, proof label, proof type, authenticated report URL. It contains no internal hashes/source IDs, staff IDs, raw notes, or signed URLs; internal consistency hashes stay server-side except a response checksum. Empty fields remain empty; no-data cells are excluded and documented. No synthetic `no_published_enrollment` rows are emitted.

Generate one bounded database snapshot (maximum proposed 100,000 rows and 25 MiB UTF-8). If it exceeds either limit, return 413 with no partial artifact. Build the complete CSV before delivery, then perform a fresh auth/grant/publication check immediately before streaming. The download response is authenticated and direct; there are no signed URLs, internal notes, SSN, DOB, home contact data, or reusable storage artifacts. Escape formulas/control characters and newlines safely. Preserve leading-zero identifiers by documenting import-as-text semantics and prefixing dangerous values as text without emitting a formula such as `="ID"`.

Nitro/Vercel must stream the finished response. The proposed 25 MiB cap exceeds Vercel’s documented 4.5 MB buffered-response constraint; use the official [limits](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/limitations) and [streaming guidance](https://vercel.com/kb/guide/how-to-bypass-vercel-body-size-limit-serverless-functions). Hosted verification must prove a >4.5 MB stream, client length/hash validation before Save, timeout behavior, and no partial bytes after failed authorization.

Providers, facilities, and a sample case

Four screens. Design rules: A→Z by default on every list, everything about a provider on one page, inline field editing (no monolithic edit form), facilities added right on the record — fixing a real defect in today's app, where saving the provider edit form silently drops facility assignments — and the same search/sort/filter treatment on the facilities list. Screen 4 is one case, mid-story.

The provider record
Attesting stamps every field the fill carried, so freshness becomes a real state instead of a guess. A pulled gap writes that one field — and the next payer fill goes from 17 of 18 to 18 of 18.
If you read one thing
Seams 1–3 are the loop that pays for itself: work arrives resolved, gets done, and records itself — no re-entry in the app. They need almost no new backend. Seams 4–6 are what stop the system getting more expensive with every payer, and they need the two new write paths.

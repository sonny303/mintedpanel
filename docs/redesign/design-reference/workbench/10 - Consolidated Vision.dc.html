Where the continuous improvement comes from
Today payer 60 costs the same as payer 6 — every setup starts from a blank grid, and the coordinator is the only place the knowledge lives. If labels live on the spine, each payer makes the next one cheaper. This is the part nothing in the current product does.
01
Every mapping teaches the next
"Individual Practitioner ID" resolved to NPI once. The next payer that says something similar arrives pre-matched, with the count of payers it was learned from as the reason.
02
Every drift repair hardens the form
A selector that broke once and was fixed becomes a known-fragile field. Coverage checks watch it first instead of finding it again on a real submission.
03
Every attestation dates the data
Pushing our record to CAQH and attesting stamps every field it carried. Stale stops being invisible without anyone running a cleanup project.
04
Every submission proves the path
A form that filled cleanly and was accepted is evidence, not a guess. First-pass rate per payer becomes the number that tells you which payers to trust unattended.
What collapses
Was Becomes
Journey 1 — get to work The panel opens on what is next. Handoff, search and the queue are three ways of changing what is in hand, not three journeys.
Journeys 2 and 3 — copy, fill One offer that scales to the page. A proven form leads with fill; anything else leads with the values.
Journey 4 — progress No tab. Finishing the work is the progress — the panel infers it and asks for one confirmation.
Journey 5 — the capture ladder Register, capture, map, repair and prove stop being five modes. An unknown form proposes its own matches; a broken one proposes its repairs.
Journey 6 — keep CAQH current CAQH is a destination, not a source: fill it from our record and attest. A gap only CAQH holds can be pulled back, which is rare.
What this asks of the backend, beyond what's in motion. Three things the current API set does not cover: a label-to-field learning store so a mapping made on one payer can be proposed on another (with the payer count as its evidence); a per-field last-verified timestamp so freshness is a real state rather than an inference; and an attestation write, so recording a CAQH attestation stamps the fields it carried and sets CAQH last attested date. Everything else in this vision rides what you already have in flight.
